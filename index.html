HOW THE PROJECT CONNECTS

From an idea to a finished product.

The proposed workflow connects specialist production, practical learning, assembly, exhibition and sales.

01✎

Plan & specify

The owner coordinates the project, design specifications and overall priorities.

02⌁

Make & produce

The master tailor produces custom textiles; the furniture-making partner manufactures furniture to agreed measurements.

03⚒

Assemble & learn

Victor and the team coordinate handling and assembly, with practical training opportunities for helpers.

04▧

Display & sell

Completed products can be displayed, offered for sale and connected to customer orders.

FUNDING & ORGANISATIONAL RELATIONSHIP

Building a project with a purpose.

The project plan reflects the founder's proposed investment in setting up and operating the business and the intended relationship between the commercial company and the nonprofit initiative.

01

Founder investment

Investment in setup, equipment, stock, operations and other agreed project requirements.

02

Commercial activity

Product sales and services provide the commercial side of the project, subject to the final business structure.

03

Job creation measures

Reinvestment of earnings into agreed training and job creation measures is a proposed arrangement, to be formalised with transparent financial and legal governance.

The final legal entities, ownership, funding terms, accounting, and any transfer or reinvestment of earnings must be confirmed by the project founders and appropriate advisers before being represented as a binding arrangement.
